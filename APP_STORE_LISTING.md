## Description (4000 chars max)

**The solitaire game that rewards your skill with real cash.**

Mega Magpie Solitaire is the beautifully crafted card game where your skill earns real rewards. Play classic Klondike solitaire, level up your character, and collect cash milestones — every win brings you closer to a real payout.

**WIN REAL CASH**
Every 10 wins unlocks a cash milestone payout. No chance involved — just pure card-playing skill. Reach Level 10 and claim a £25 reward. Your winnings track in your personal wallet and pay out to your bank once payments go live.

**PLAY YOUR WAY**
• Classic Klondike solitaire with smooth, satisfying card animations
• Hint and undo boosts to sharpen your play
• Auto-finish when you've cracked the game
• Speed Match — race the clock for bonus points and prizes
• Lucky Pick — earn free tokens just by playing

**COLLECT & CUSTOMISE**
• Choose from a cast of unique characters, each with their own outfits
• Unlock beautiful card back designs
• Level up from Rookie to Legend across 10 tiers
• Daily login streak rewards — the longer you play, the bigger the bonus

**EARN FREE COINS & TOKENS**
• Daily login rewards keep your balance growing
• Watch optional ads for bonus tokens
• Token cashback on wins
• Spend coins on hints, undos, and Lucky Picks

**STAKE FOR BIGGER REWARDS**
Before each game, choose your stake level — from Play Safe (no stake) right up to Whale (10x multiplier). Win the game and collect your multiplied reward. No stake, no risk — entirely your choice.

**RESPONSIBLE PLAY FIRST**
• Age-verified 18+ play
• Spending warnings built in
• Session and lifetime spend tracking
• Take a break any time via the Play Safe screen
• Linked to BeGambleAware.org for support

Mega Magpie Solitaire is free to play. Coin packs are available for purchase. Cash milestone payouts require payment integration (coming soon).
